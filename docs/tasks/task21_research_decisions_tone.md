## The user's concern (both parts serve it)
LLM output is averaged not only in wording but in content: decisions, facts, actions come out as the most typical choice; contrast and rare-but-real choices almost never happen; the model's tone shows through any system prompt. The goal is text and a world that feel like the source author (Sapkowski for the Witcher), not like an assistant. The engine is world-agnostic: any fix must work from a world's own reference texts. Your training data is outdated: search live, give links and dates for every claim.

## 21A — the decision layer (budget 0.30 USD for probes)
The user's idea to critique and complete: route every decision that shapes tone, emotion, replies and world reactions to a dedicated decision model (fast, cheap, smart), possibly fine-tuned, local or via API. Known weak points (from the lead): decision models are trained on the same averaged data; they pick only among listed options, so who writes the options matters; "illogical" human choices come from a specific person's state (pride, fatigue, a false belief), not from randomness; fine-tuning availability is unknown.
Find out:
- Current decision/classifier models usable cheaply (OpenRouter decision endpoint and others), and which can be fine-tuned by us via API (provider, method, minimum data, price, how the tuned model is served and at what cost). Same for local: what fits 8 GB VRAM shared with a desktop compositor (sizes, LoRA/QLoRA tooling current versions, training time on an RTX 3070 laptop).
- Research 2024–2026 on mode collapse / homogenisation of LLM decisions and remedies (e.g. sampling from verbalised distributions, persona and state conditioning, option generation from corpora, contrastive decoding). What has measured evidence; what failed.
- Prior art on predicting a fictional character's actual choice in a novel ("character decision prediction", role-play fidelity benchmarks) and what accuracy current models reach vs humans.
- Design a concrete first experiment for us: decision points from our ingested pilot arc (`harness/canon/`, store under `campaigns/canon/`, never commit book text) — situation, options (including what the book character actually did), and how to measure averaging (how often each method picks the book-like vs the typical option; distribution over options across samples). A probe of 10–20 points with our current decider (`liquid/d1-ultra`) and LLM (`openai/gpt-6-luna`) is allowed within budget; grade yourself.
- Ranked recommendations: adopt / test next / ignore, with cost now vs later.
