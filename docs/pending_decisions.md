# Pending decisions (agreed in chat, not yet in decisions.md)

Agreed in chat on 2026-10-19, to be written into `docs/decisions.md` in one pass when the discussion ends (HANDOFF rule: no incremental edits). Delete this memory once written.

1. **Background knowledge.** A character knows what follows from their life (age, occupation and years in it, place, standing, connections): a child little, a 10-year innkeeper locals, prices and many (distorted) rumours, an aristocrat court and politics. Computed lazily when a topic comes up, stored as a knowledge record with source "own life" and an in-world time, so it stays consistent. Rumour versions come from what reached that place. The NPC output check asks "any channel: perceived, told, or plausible from their life", not only "was told". Secrets still do not leak.
2. **Player character inner state.** The world sets body (wounds, pain as a signal, poison, fatigue, intoxication, illness), senses (incl. hallucinations) and external facts. Emotions, impact, trauma, attitudes, meaning are the player's only; the narrator never assigns them. Inner state in the panel comes only from the player. NPCs react to observable behaviour. Turn assistant suggests actions, not feelings. Replaces "fear" in the current "world can affect the character" line.
3. **Mind magic** (Yennefer on Geralt, the djinn wish) is a world law like poison, only from an explicit in-world cause, recorded as a timed effect. Resistance is judged per situation (caster power, target nature, circumstances), not a fixed rule. While under it the player still writes, as resistance attempts and remnants of own consciousness; how much breaks through depends on the spell's strength (strong spell: the body follows the caster, narrator describes it, player has inner voice and rare breakthroughs). Feelings induced by the spell are part of the effect; after it ends the player's attitude is theirs again.
4. **NPC claims.** What an NPC says is stored as "X said Y" (an asserted claim), never as world truth; truth is a separate record. If the world has not decided the matter yet, truth is settled lazily when it matters (e.g. the player arrives), by plausibility: what is known about the speaker, their source, the regional situation.
5. **Narrator output check.** Narrator text is checked for knowledge leaks too (names the player character does not know, things the character cannot perceive), with the first-line decider, run in parallel with the rest of the turn.
6. **Retroactive-interrupt window** closes as soon as the next event starts (player sends a turn, another NPC speaks, or the world acts). Until then the last NPC utterance is not final: its facts are stored but can be rolled back.
7. **Record language** does not matter to the user; what matters is that names never get distorted. Canonical Ukrainian forms (from the Ukrainian translation) via a name glossary applied at write time; names are kept consistent over long play (task 10: English records + Ukrainian names works for luna).
8. **Book ingestion is piloted on one arc of book 1** (chosen: book "Останнє бажання", frame "Голос розуму-1" + story "Відьмак"; task 13), fully checked record by record, then played to see if it behaves like a real simulation; iterate until the user is satisfied, then the rest of book 1, then the whole saga (cost reasons).
9. **Model-error for an NPC knowledge leak:** player presses the button and says the character could not know it; the engine checks the channel: no channel → regenerate and remember; channel exists → tell the player how the character knows (agreed: channel type in play, full event behind a "show (spoiler)" tap and in debug).
10. **No patterns in player-facing text:** the user does not want a rigid JSON contract to make messages formulaic. Proposed: narrator and NPCs write free prose only; speakers and observable actions are derived by extractor/decider; a repetition metric across turns; the user flags patterns in play.
11. **Corrections of old records** are corrections (record marked wrong, derived ones stale and recomputed lazily), not rewinds; the player's choices stay. User agreed "mostly" on 2026-10-19.
12. **Scene modes (replaces "turn-based vs flowing, compare both").** Base mode: no queue or rounds; a scene director decides after each event who (if anyone) speaks or acts, from character, situation, who was addressed, interest; silence is possible; the world waits for the player as long as needed. Live scene: a toggle button (on/off mid-scene), real time, nobody waits; characters may arrive, leave on their business, get distracted; the player presses "Говорю" to speak/act; silence when addressed is in-world silence and NPCs react. While the player types after "Говорю", the world gives a natural pause (an impatient NPC may cut in); pause length tuned in play. Live scene task comes after task 12 (builds on its director).
