## Who may know what (for the knower check)
- Only the people in the room know a scene. The tavern witnesses (innkeeper, guests, the three guards) know the killing; Velerad learns it from the guards (not shown) and states "троє убитих" himself.
- Foltest was not in the yard: he never learns of Ostrit's bribe in the text. Velerad and Segelin never hear Ostrit's confession.
- Nobody but Geralt knows what happened in the crypt; Velerad learns only the result.
- Geralt learns the history of the princess from Velerad (day 1 evening), not before.
- Segelin and Ostrit know the soldier and miller facts; the king is told the cure by Geralt (day 2).
